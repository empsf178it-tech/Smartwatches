Brand Story

TECHNOLOGY THAT MOVES WITH YOU

PULSEON was founded on a simple premise: smartwatches should not compromise between luxurious horology aesthetics and medical-grade bio-telemetry.

Design Ethos

Aesthetic Precision Meets Aerospace Alloys

We select Grade-5 titanium for lightness and strength, paired with scratch-resistant sapphire crystal glass. Every curve, bevel, and digital crown knurling is sculpted for ergonomic wrist comfort.

Health Mission

Empowering Daily Wellness

Our proprietary PPG sensor arrays capture continuous physiological metrics, giving you action-oriented health insights without distracting from your day.
